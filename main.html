Ever spent hours downloading a massive modpack, only to realize two hours in that it’s not your style, horribly unoptimized, or full of game-breaking bugs? We’ve all been there.

My goal here is simple: to save you time and help you find your next favorite Minecraft adventure. Whether you are looking for hardcore tech progression, magic, RPG-style questing, or lightweight packs for low-end PCs, I test them out so you know exactly what to expect before hitting download.

What can you look forward to?

Weekly reviews of different modpacks, that I test based performance, content, main audience, and progression.
